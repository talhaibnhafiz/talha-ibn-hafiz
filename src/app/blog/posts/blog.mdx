## Main Post

Traditional computing relies on bits that are either 0 or 1. Quantum computing, however, utilizes qubits ($|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$), which can exist in multiple states simultaneously due to Superposition.The Engineering Challenge: Maintaining "Quantum Coherence." Qubits are extremely sensitive; even a slight change in temperature or vibration can cause errors.The Impact: Once we achieve "Quantum Supremacy," we can simulate molecular interactions at an atomic level, leading to the discovery of new materials and life-saving drugs in days rather than decades.
