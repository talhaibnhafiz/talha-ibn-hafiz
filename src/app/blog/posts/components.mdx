## Main Post
Generative AI, such as GPT-4 or Claude, uses Transformers—a type of neural network architecture—to understand the context of data. Unlike traditional AI that simply classifies data (e.g., "is this a cat?"), Generative AI creates new content.

The Technical Shift: We are moving from supervised learning to Self-Supervised Learning, where models train on massive datasets without human labeling.

The Future: The next step is Multimodal AI, which seamlessly integrates text, image, video, and audio into a single reasoning engine.
