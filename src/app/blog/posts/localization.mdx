## Main Post
While most hydrogen is produced from natural gas (Grey Hydrogen), Green Hydrogen is created using Electrolysis. This process uses renewable electricity (wind or solar) to split water ($H_2O$) into hydrogen and oxygen.
Engineering Hurdles: Storage and transport. Hydrogen is the lightest element and requires high-pressure tanks or cryogenic temperatures to stay liquid.

Why it Matters: It is the only viable way to decarbonize "hard-to-abate" sectors like steel manufacturing, heavy shipping, and long-haul aviation.
