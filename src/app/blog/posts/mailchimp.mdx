## Main Post
As we move toward 6G and billions of IoT devices, "Cloud Computing" becomes a bottleneck. Edge Computing brings the processing power closer to the data source (like a camera or a sensor) rather than a distant data center.The Benefit: Ultra-low latency. For a self-driving car, a $100$-millisecond delay in identifying a pedestrian can be the difference between safety and an accident.Security: By processing data locally, sensitive information doesn't always need to travel over the internet, enhancing privacy.
