## Main Post
CRISPR is essentially a search-and-replace tool for DNA. It uses a guide RNA to find a specific genetic sequence and an enzyme (Cas9) to cut it.

The Breakthrough: In 2024 and 2025, we saw the first FDA-approved treatments for Sickle Cell Disease using CRISPR.

Synthetic Biology: Engineers are now looking at "Gene Drives" to eliminate malaria-carrying mosquitoes or to create "Bio-factories" where bacteria produce sustainable plastics.
