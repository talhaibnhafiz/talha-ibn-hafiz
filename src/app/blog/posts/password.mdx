## Main Post

The construction industry is one of the largest CO2 emitters. Engineers are now developing Bio-concrete. This material contains dormant bacteria and nutrients that activate when a crack forms and water seeps in. The bacteria produce limestone, which "heals" the crack.

The Goal: To double the lifespan of bridges and skyscrapers, drastically reducing the need for new cement production.

Circular Economy: Using recycled ocean plastics and industrial ash as structural components is becoming a standard in modern civil engineering.
