## Main Post

We are entering an era of Autonomous Cyber Defense. Traditional firewalls look for known "signatures" of viruses. AI, however, uses Anomaly Detection to spot unusual behavior—like a user logging in at 3 AM from a new location and downloading large files.

The Threat: "Deepfake" phishing. AI can now mimic a CEO's voice or face in real-time video calls to authorize fraudulent wire transfers.

The Solution: Zero-Trust Architecture, where every single request is continuously verified by AI models.
