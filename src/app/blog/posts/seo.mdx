## Main Post

The era of "disposable" rockets is over. Companies like SpaceX and Blue Origin have mastered Propulsive Landing, where the first stage of a rocket returns to Earth and lands vertically.The Math of Success: Reusing a rocket reduces the cost of a launch from $\$200$ million to under $\$20$ million.Orbital Economy: This price drop is fueling the "New Space" race, enabling mega-constellations of satellites for global high-speed internet and the early stages of asteroid mining.
