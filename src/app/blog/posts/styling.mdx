## Main Post

BCI technology aims to create a direct communication pathway between the human brain and external devices. By using tiny electrodes implanted in the motor cortex, AI algorithms can "decode" neural signals and translate thoughts into digital commands.

The Engineering Challenge: Bio-compatibility. The human body often rejects foreign objects, so engineers must develop "soft" electrodes that don't cause scarring in brain tissue.

The Impact: This technology is currently helping paralyzed individuals control cursors or robotic limbs with their minds, with the long-term goal of treating neurological conditions like Parkinson’s.
